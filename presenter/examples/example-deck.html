Why households matter

Most business owners are married — about 70% in the US — yet we model them as lone decision-makers.
A spouse's salary is household insurance.
It cushions consumption when the firm does badly.
But that insurance is itself risky: spouses lose jobs in recessions, exactly when firms are also distressed.
